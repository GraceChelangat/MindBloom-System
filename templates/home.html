ABOUT MINDBLOOM

A safe space to understand your journey.

MindBloom is designed for teenagers and young adults living with anorexia nervosa. It helps you reflect on mood, eating behaviours and everyday well-being, so small changes become easier to notice.

Learn more +

The system brings reflections related to anorexia nervosa together to support awareness, early support and meaningful conversations with healthcare professionals.

Private handled with care Supportive never judgemental Simple one check-in at a time

MindBloom is a monitoring tool, not a diagnostic service or replacement for professional care.
